Keepy Up in Motion

Two short animations of the finished game: one webcam on a stand, Cozmo and MiP roaming the room, and a projected line on the wall that follows the balloon. Sound plays only for a tap and for a landing.

The robots follow the balloon. Cozmo follows it in the left half of the room and MiP in the right, and when it lands the nearer robot nudges it back toward a child. The robots don't hit the balloon: the children keep it in the air.
